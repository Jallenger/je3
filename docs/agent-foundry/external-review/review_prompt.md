You are reviewing the design for **Agent Foundry**, a supervised system where competing AI "guilds" research, test and run small lawful businesses with a human Chairman supplying capital and approvals. It is presented as a turn-based strategy game. Two documents follow:

1. **The build brief** (`agent_foundry_build_spec_v3.1_lean.md`): the authoritative spec, including budget stages, evidence rules, funding operations and the game layer.
2. **The game UI mockup write-up** (`agent_foundry_game_ui_mockup.md`): a screen-by-screen description of the current visual design.

Please read both carefully, then respond in Markdown with these sections:

## 1. Overall verdict
Three to five sentences. Is this buildable and worth building as specified? What is the single biggest risk?

## 2. Strengths
What the design gets right and should keep. Be specific: cite section numbers.

## 3. Problems and gaps
Ranked, most serious first. For each: what's wrong, why it matters in the real world (money, legal exposure, wasted effort, misleading the Chairman), and a concrete fix. Pay particular attention to:
- whether the A$20 / A$45 / A$75 staged budget is realistic for what's asked;
- the evidence-integrity rules (§7) and whether they can be gamed;
- funding operations (§14A): custody, hard caps, reconciliation, the purchase handoff;
- whether the game layer could mislead the Chairman about real-world consequences;
- anything legally risky for an Australia-based individual operator.

## 4. Your world idea
Propose **your own concept for the game world**: how the Foundry should look and feel as a game. Cover:
- the setting and metaphor (keep the current one, change it, or replace it, and why);
- the map: what the territories, buildings, routes and figures represent, and how guild dominance is shown;
- how a turn feels to play, from the Chairman's point of view;
- three to five signature moments or mechanics that would make it memorable;
- how your world keeps real-world consequences (real money, real people contacted) obvious and never hidden behind the theme;
- what you would cut from the current mockup.

Your world must follow the brief's rules: every game element maps to a real record, plain words come first, there's no pressure to spend, and agents never see the game.

## 5. Top 5 changes you'd make to the brief
A numbered list, each a single actionable sentence.
